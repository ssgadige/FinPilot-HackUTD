FP-14 planning boundary

POST/PATCH/DELETE goals and subscription preferences use authenticated identity.
DELETE goals, subscription changes, and plan acceptance require confirmation=true.
No endpoint accepts a caller's proposal payload or user identity.

Internal Planning.propose accepts only a completed, current, owned run and a
validated PlanPayload. Payload fields: allocations (kind=goal_contribution or
extra_debt_payment, target_id, monthly_cents, day_of_month 1..28), reserve_cents,
evidence_ids, assumptions. Goal targets must be owned; debt targets must be
selected linked liabilities; evidence must be accessible at the same snapshot.
Contributions reserve liquid cash; they never move money or increase saved_cents.
Extra debt payments supplement scheduled minimums, which remain in the baseline.

POST /scenarios accepts plan_id and applies these allocations on future monthly
due dates once. It returns hypothetical dated balances and persisted calculation
evidence. Stale or disconnected plan inputs require a revision (409).

Plans become accepted only through POST /plans/:id/accept with expected_version,
confirmation=true and an idempotency key. One SQLite transaction supersedes the
old accepted plan and accepts its replacement; failures roll everything back.
A proposal or failed revision never silently replaces an accepted plan.
Goal/preferences/data changes make old snapshots visibly stale; their payloads
are withheld from reads. Disconnect also revokes dependent history access.

Offer retrieval is injected through the server-only OfferResolver interface.
Unknown/unavailable integrations fail explicitly. Chosen offers and cancellation
intentions remain planned external actions; realized_savings_cents is always zero.
